Diktafonas AI Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026

Diktafonas AI is designed to process meeting recordings privately on your device.

Data processed on your device

Audio recordings, transcripts, speaker profiles, names, optional email addresses, summaries, and app settings are stored on your device. Audio transcription and speaker processing run locally. Recordings and models are excluded from iCloud backups. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain for this device only.

Optional AI processing

When you explicitly request an AI summary or email rewrite, the app sends transcript or draft text—not the audio recording—to the AI provider you selected (OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic) using your own API key. The provider processes that text under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not operate an intermediary server.

Calendar, email, and downloads

With your permission, the app may read relevant calendar event details and create events. Email drafts are opened in the iOS mail or share interface and are sent only when you choose to send them. Speech and speaker-recognition model files may be downloaded from the model publishers.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can delete recordings, transcripts, speaker profiles, and API keys from the app. Removing the app deletes its local data, subject to normal iOS behavior.
